PACK QUICK GUIDE

What is a Pack?

A Pack is a set of short reference voice clips used as prompts during a round. Players listen, perform the line into the microphone, and hear the result.

Put one in the game

  1. 01Get the filesDownload a Pack from its creator page.
  2. 02Import it hereChoose the ZIP or extracted folder below.
  3. 03Install and playThe installer opens the game bridge and copies validated audio into this browser.
  4. 04Start the roundThe new Pack is refreshed and selected in Custom Voice Packs automatically.
