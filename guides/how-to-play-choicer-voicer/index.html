CHOICER VOICER GUIDE

How to play The Choicer Voicer online

Start the browser game, grant microphone access, and understand the performance loop.

01

Before you start

Connect the microphone you intend to use before opening the game. A current desktop version of Chrome or Edge provides the clearest permission controls. Headphones reduce echo, while a quiet room makes playback easier to understand.

02

The three-part loop

Listen to the prompt, choose a voice or delivery, and commit to the recording. Let the complete replay finish before changing settings. The game works best when a room treats imperfect performances as part of the entertainment.

03

Keep the session moving

Show a new player one complete turn instead of explaining every possibility. In a group, announce who is next and keep the active speaker close to the selected microphone.
