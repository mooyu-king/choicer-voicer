PACK BASICS

What is a Choicer Voicer Pack?

A Choicer Voicer Pack is a set of playable voice material prepared for the game. Depending on the creator and archive, it may include timed audio clips, labels, and other files used to present a different scene or voice choice. A Pack page should identify its creator and original source; a public download does not by itself grant permission to redistribute the contents.

INSTALL

How community Packs work

Choose a Pack, inspect its source, and use the guided loader when a verified archive is available. The browser downloads and checks supported files, stores the playable clips on this device, then uses the Pack Bridge to select them in the embedded game. If a source cannot be loaded automatically, follow the manual import instructions on its detail page.

CREATE

Community Pack or custom Pack?

Community Packs are credited third-party releases you use as supplied. The custom workbench is for permitted media you provide yourself: upload a local video or audio file, identify spoken sections, choose the clips you need, and render a personal Pack without uploading the source media to this site.
