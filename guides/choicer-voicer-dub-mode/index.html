CHOICER VOICER GUIDE

The Choicer Voicer Dub Mode guide

Prepare a source, assign performers, rehearse timing, and turn the best take into a clip.

01

Prepare the scene

Use only media you created, licensed, or received permission to transform. Note the duration, characters, and lines before recording.

02

Choose continuity over perfection

Watch once, rehearse once, then record a complete pass. Small mistakes often feel more alive than a sequence of disconnected perfect lines.

03

Review with a purpose

Compare timing, clarity, energy, and chemistry. Import the selected recording into the local Dub Studio to mark a strong section and prepare a caption.
