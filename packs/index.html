HOW TO USE A PACK

From files to the next round.

Both Pack paths use the same browser game installer.

  1. 01Prepare the PackDownload a community ZIP or create clips from your own media.
  2. 02Import or renderValidate the downloaded files, or render your selected custom lines.
  3. 03Start the game bridgeWait until the connection indicator reports that the Pack Bridge is ready.
  4. 04Install and playSend the Pack, open Custom Voice Packs, and use it for the next round.
