HOW TO PLAY / COMMUNITY PACKS

From card to round.

Every verified community Pack opens a guided game page. The browser handles the download and Pack Bridge handoff before you start playing.

  1. 01Open a PackSelect View Pack for details, or Load & Play to start the guided flow.
  2. 02Download automaticallyThe archive is fetched in the Pack page and its progress stays visible.
  3. 03Load into the gameThe browser unpacks the clips and sends them through the Pack Bridge.
  4. 04Play and recordWhen Play Now appears, start a round, record your reactions, and share the result.
