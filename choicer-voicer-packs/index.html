From files to the next round.

Both Pack paths use the same browser game installer.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Custom Pack media is processed in your browser. Community downloads remain credited to their source creators; source-site permissions still apply.

WHAT IS A PACK?

Change the material, keep the performance loop.

A Choicer Voicer Pack supplies voice material for a new round. Community Packs come from credited third-party creators; custom Packs are made locally from media you are allowed to process. In both cases, the Pack is prepared, checked, installed through the browser bridge, and selected inside the game.

COMMUNITY OR CUSTOM

Choose the path that matches your source.

Use the community directory when you want a ready-made scene with a visible creator and source. Use the custom workbench when you already have permitted video or audio and want to select specific spoken moments. The custom workflow processes source media on this device.
